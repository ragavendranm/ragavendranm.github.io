### Reading the tea leaves for the AI era

Two good articles around what we can expect in the AI era.

First, Ben Thompson on the upheaval coming for the internet economy in [“The Agentic AI and the Original Sin”](https://stratechery.com/2025/the-agentic-web-and-original-sin/). I always love how Thompson brings some historical perspective in his Articles, and this one is no exception.

Here’s how Thompson himself summarizes his article.

> *Will AI Kill the Web, or Save It? The original web that we know and love was the human web; that’s why advertising was the preferred business model, and Google was the big winner. So what happens when AI Agents are the preferred means to gather information? AI’s don’t care about ads, which means the web needs a new business model; I’ve been a long-time critic of micro-transactions, because they are anti-human, but they might be the solution to an AI-mediated future. Or, to put it another way, [AI won’t just save AR and VR, it might save crypto as well](https://stratechery.com/2025/the-agentic-web-and-original-sin/). — Ben Thompson*

The second one is from Benn Stancil, who argues [“The ads are coming”](https://benn.substack.com/p/the-ads-are-coming?utm_source=substack&utm_campaign=post_embed&utm_medium=web). This paragraph resonated with me in particular.

> *If an AI company gives us enough free stuff—if OpenAI makes Jony Ive’s unimaginable technology free, provided that you leave ad trackers on—plenty of us will blindly accept its cookies as the price of a new generation of technology. So the advertising products are there; the only question is how ruthlessly AI vendors choose to chase it.*
